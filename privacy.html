Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Purpose

Rclone Research Sync is a personal-use configuration for synchronizing selected files between Google Drive and a user-controlled Linux environment.

Google Drive Data

With the account owner's permission, Rclone can read, download, upload, modify, and delete files according to the synchronization settings.

Data Storage and Sharing

Synchronized files may be stored on the Linux server and Google Drive. OAuth credentials are kept in the local Rclone configuration and are not published on this website.

This is a personal-use configuration, not a public service. Google Drive data is not sold or shared with third parties by this configuration. Authorized external services, if added later, will be assessed separately.

Security and Revocation

The account owner may revoke application access through Google Account settings. Local copies of synchronized files must be deleted separately when no longer needed.

Contact

Questions may be directed to the application owner using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
